OpenRouter Credit Monitor

Monitor your OpenRouter API credits with background alerts.

What it does

  • Fetches your OpenRouter credit balance with your own Management Key
  • Background checks every 15–60 minutes (you choose)
  • Local notification only when the balance changes
  • History of the last 30 checks, stored on your device

Requirements

You need your own OpenRouter Management Key:
openrouter.ai/settings/management-keys

Paste it in the app's Settings, set the interval, and pull to refresh.
