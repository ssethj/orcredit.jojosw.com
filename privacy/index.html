Privacy Policy — OpenRouter Credit Monitor

Effective: September 2026

Data stored on your device only

  • OpenRouter Management Key: encrypted with AndroidKeyStore. It never leaves the device except to call the OpenRouter API (api.openrouter.ai) to fetch your credit balance at your request. No backup. Never transmitted to us or anyone else.
  • Settings and history: refresh interval, last 30 balance checks, monitoring flag, remove-ads purchase flag. Local only.

Data shared with third parties

  • Google AdMob: advertising ID and ad interaction data, for banner ads. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Personalized ads follow your device consent choice, changeable anytime via Settings > Privacy options in the app.
  • Google Play Billing: purchase tokens for the remove-ads product, processed by Google.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash stack traces, device model, OS version, installation IDs. No API key, no balance data, no user ID attached.

Permissions used

  • Internet — OpenRouter API calls and ads
  • Notifications (optional) — balance alerts
  • Billing — remove-ads purchase
  • Run at startup — resume monitoring after reboot or app update

Deletion

Use in-app Settings to clear your key. Uninstalling the app wipes all local data. No accounts exist on our side, so there is nothing to delete server-side — we hold no user data at all.

Children

This app requires the user's own OpenRouter API management key (a developer account relationship) and is not directed at children.
